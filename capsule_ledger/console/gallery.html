Rule 3 · degradation shown, not hidden

Assurance rungs

Classes and rungs are orthogonal. Climbing is normal life (animate once, settle); a downgrade shows the rung it holds, never the one it hoped for.

Standalone
ledger_mode: standalone
The record is signed and complete on its own. A stranger must trust the operator that it was never replaced.
Countersigned
attestation: counterparty
The other side of the action signed it too. Neither party can now tell a different story.
Locally receipted
ledger_mode: chained
The record holds its place in an unbroken sequence. Removing or editing it would visibly break the chain.
Counterparty-visible
shared checkpoint
The sequence summary is held by the counterparty as well — history can't be rewritten without the other side noticing.

A summary was lodged with independent public logs — this history cannot be quietly rewritten afterwards.

Rule 5 · precision, never a generic red X

When verification fails

Failure red is #A93F2B — reserved exclusively for verification failure. Nothing else on any surface may use it. A failed record names the stage that failed.

✕digest_mismatch
The contents changed
"This record does not match its fingerprint — it is not the record that was sealed." The mismatching field group is named; the rest of the bundle keeps its own verdicts.
✕signature_invalid
The signature doesn't hold
"The signature on this record was not made by the key it names." Renders who it claims to be from, and that the claim fails — never just a red icon.
✕chain_gap
A break in the sequence
"Records are missing between #47 (Tue 14:02) and #48 (Tue 14:19)." The gap window is the finding — located in time and position, browsable from either edge.
✕inclusion_unproven
No independent witness
"This record claims to be witnessed, but the log's receipt doesn't check out (or can't be reached — retry when online)." Distinguishes unreachable from disproven, always.

The verify ritual

Four stage rows. Pass is sage, fail is brick (the only other permitted brick use), skipped is a neutral outline — never a silent green dot, never a bare red X.
